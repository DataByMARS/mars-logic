About MARS

The research partner behind better decisions.

Modern Analytics and Research Solutions exists for one reason: to give teams research they can actually trust — and stand behind when it counts.

MARS was built on a simple frustration: too much "research" is produced to justify a decision that's already been made.

We do the opposite. We start from the question you genuinely need answered, design a study capable of answering it, and follow the evidence wherever it leads. That means being honest about uncertainty, transparent about method, and rigorous about the difference between what the data shows and what we'd like it to show.

The name says what we are — Modern Analytics and Research Solutions. Modern analytics, because the questions worth asking today deserve current methods, not recycled templates. Research, because evidence is the point. Solutions, because a finding that no one can act on isn't finished.

How we work

A study moves through four stages.

Each stage protects the one before it, so the question you started with is the question the report answers.

STAGE 01

Design

Define the question, choose the method, and set the standard of evidence up front.

STAGE 02

Collect

Build and field instruments that produce clean, reliable, well-documented data.

STAGE 03

Analyze

Run the analysis transparently, checking every result against the original question.

STAGE 04

Report

Visualize and write up findings so the right people can act with confidence.
